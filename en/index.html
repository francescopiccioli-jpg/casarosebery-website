I · The Steakhouse

Eleven origins,
one grill

We select our cuts from the finest farms in the world and dry-age them in house for as long as it takes for the meat to gain tenderness and depth of flavour. Then the grill does the rest: few gestures, no shortcuts.

How it works

Choose your cut

Bone-in or boneless, from organic Marchigiana to Japanese Wagyu. Prices are per 100 g: our staff will help you find the right weight for your table.

Dry-ageing

Every cut matures in our ageing room until it reaches its ideal point, for more tender meat and a deeper flavour.

The cooking

Over the grill, to the doneness we recommend for each cut. Complete it with a side, our raw bar or Iberian charcuterie.

Posillipo, Naples

A timeless
setting

Casa Rosebery was born in Posillipo, one of the most enchanting spots on the Bay of Naples. A restaurant where elegant surroundings meet a passion for authentic cooking — every detail designed to make each visit unforgettable.

Our name recalls Villa Rosebery, the residence of the President of the Italian Republic just a few steps away: history, beauty and refinement as the foundations of everything we do.

This stretch of coast has a long history at the table: from 1889, for generations, it was home to Giuseppone a Mare, one of the best-loved seafood restaurants in Naples, so much so that many Neapolitans still call Riva Fiorita simply "Giuseppone". Casa Rosebery is a new venture, under different management and with a different cuisine, the grill, but with the same sea in front of it.
